Privacy policy

Shiosai: Binaural Beats, last updated November 2026

Shiosai collects no data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Every tone and background sound is generated on your iPhone while you listen. Your choices (backgrounds, volumes, timers) are saved only on your iPhone and never sent to us or to anyone else. Your device backups may include them, depending on your Apple settings.

Melodies

The app reads the list of melodies from Shiosai's file host (Vercel Blob), and new melodies download from there when you first play them. These requests ask only for those files: they carry no account, name or device identifier. Like any web request it shows the host your IP address; we don't use or keep it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, contact or payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

Children

Shiosai collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date. Questions: nico@kind-labs.dev.

Integritetspolicy

Shiosai samlar inte in några uppgifter. Inget konto, ingen reklam, ingen analys och ingen spårning. Alla toner och bakgrundsljud skapas på din iPhone medan du lyssnar, och dina val sparas bara där. Nya melodier laddas ner från Shiosais filserver (Vercel Blob) utan konto eller enhets-id; som vid alla webbförfrågningar ser servern din IP-adress, som vi inte använder eller sparar. Prenumerationer hanteras av Apple; vi får aldrig dina kontakt- eller betalningsuppgifter. Frågor: nico@kind-labs.dev.
