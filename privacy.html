Privacy policy

Shiosai: Binaural Beats, effective October 25, 2026

Shiosai collects no data. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Every tone and sound is generated on your iPhone while you listen. Your choices (backgrounds, volumes, timers) are saved only on your iPhone and never sent to us or to anyone else. Your device backups may include them, depending on your Apple settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, contact or payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

Children

Shiosai collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date. Questions: nicoamoretti@gmail.com.
