Shiosai: Binaural Beats

Binaural beats, isochronic tones, melodies, ocean, rain and noise for relaxing, focusing and falling asleep. The tones and background sounds are made on your iPhone as you listen.

Questions

Do I need headphones?
For binaural beats, yes: each ear hears its own tone and the beat forms between them. Without headphones Shiosai switches to isochronic tones, a single tone that pulses, which works on the speaker.
How loud should it be?
Quiet. Start low and stay well below the level you would use for music.
Is it for everyone?
Don't listen while driving or doing anything that needs your attention. If you have epilepsy or a history of seizures, ask your doctor before listening to rhythmic tones. Shiosai is for relaxing, focusing and sleep; it is not a medical treatment.
Does it play with the screen locked?
Yes, with its controls on the lock screen. The sleep timer fades it out.
How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Shiosai. Or in the app: Settings, Manage subscription.
I changed phones. How do I get Pro back?
In the app: Settings, Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
